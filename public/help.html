a little help

keep the good bits.

something not working?

Report a bug on GitHub. Include your phone model, browser and the steps that caused the problem. A GitHub account is required.

Reports are public. Leave out names, account emails, couple addresses, invitation links, photos, passwords and recovery links. We do not have a private support inbox or guaranteed response time yet.

your photos.

Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB. HEIC is not supported; export it as JPG first. Photos are resized before saving. If an image is still too large, choose a smaller one.

Each space holds up to 50 active memories. Move one to trash to make room. Memories can be restored for 30 days, provided there is space. After that, they are removed from the live database. Hosting recovery copies may persist under the provider’s retention policy.

save your little ticket.

Tap “save date ticket” or “save our date ticket”. A PNG download and an image preview will open. On a phone, tap “save ticket” or press and hold the preview to save it to Photos. Where supported, you can use “share ticket”.

Tickets use the latest plan when generated. A ticket already saved on your phone is a snapshot; download it again after a date changes.

what’s shared?

Your couple address shows your names, cover photo, welcome and latest invitation. Anyone who knows that address can view the invitation and reply. Your account and scrapbook stay behind your sign-in.

Use “export our scrapbook” to download your notes, ratings and photos, including memories still in trash. Keep this file somewhere private. Downloaded copies are not removed when you delete a memory here.

Email verification and password reset delivery are still being set up. Keep access to your account credentials.

free means free.

Pick a Date is free to use. There is no checkout, subscription or payment collected by this tool, so there are no charges to refund.

This is an early version. Keep a separate export of memories that matter to you.

a quick check with your person.

  1. Create an invitation, then open its link on a different phone or browser where the owner is not signed in.
  2. Suggest a new day or activity. The owner should see a suggestion awaiting approval.
  3. Approve it as the owner. Both open views should update within about eight seconds when visible.
  4. Save the ticket on both phones and check the date, time and timezone.
  5. Add a photo memory, export it, move it to trash and restore it.

For a real device check, use both Android Chrome and iPhone Safari where available. Simulated screen sizes cannot confirm camera, Photos or native sharing behaviour.
